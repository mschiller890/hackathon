The OOF model predicts only 16 targets (ipp1_common.TARGETS), so HAS_PRED_BOUNDARY is false and the legend entry 'Předpověď sítě' (red dashed) matches nothing; the brief promised 'skutečný a předpovězený tvar'. Fix: in a NEW script (ipp1_common.py must not change) run the same OOF with the 12 boundary targets R_top, Z_top, R_bottom, Z_bottom, R_outer, Z_outer, R_inner, Z_inner and squareness_upper_outer, squareness_upper_inner, squareness_lower_outer, squareness_lower_inner (optionally also a, R_geom, Z_geom), append their _pred columns to out/ipp1-predictions.csv, and re-run export_viewer_data.py; the viewer detects them automatically. I tested that path in a scratch copy with synthetic 6 mm perturbations: the dashed outline draws correctly and is distinguishable from the black one (shots/predboundary_test.png). Until then, relabel the legend entry to 'Předpověď (body)'.
- 2. [BLOCKER] The vacuum wall is missing: data.js has wall null and the status bar says 'Obrys stěny: nenačten'. The limiter case therefore touches nothing on screen and the D shape floats in an empty box. Fix: get notebooks/COMPASS_stena.csv from the organizers' repository (docs/PLAN.md IMPL-10 says this needs the owner's OK; I did not download it) into data/raw/organizers/ and re-run export_viewer_data.py. The view range is then recomputed, so re-check legend placement and plasma size afterwards.

Fix: let the toolbar wrap or apply the compact rule up to about 1700 px, and move the chips and the export button into the stage panel header; test at 1366, 1440, 1512 and 1600.
- 4. [HIGH] At 1366x768 and 1024x768 the legend is drawn inside the plot and covers the upper half of the plasma (shots/p1366.png). Fix: put the legend in the left rail or under the plot, never over the data, or compute a bounding-box collision and place it in a free corner.
- 5. [HIGH] The first impression is the worst frame. - 6. [HIGH] True and predicted cannot be told apart, which is the central message. At 0.35 to 0.69 px/mm a 0.3 mm error is under half a pixel; true markers sit under the red ones. In the divertor the X marker and the two 12 px strike triangles overlap and hide the dotted legs (shots/div138_crop.png). Fix: add a magnifier inset around X-point and strike points (about 20 px/mm) with both sets visible, or an 'error x50' toggle drawing arrows from true to predicted; use smaller markers; draw the true marker as a ring on top.
- 7. [MEDIUM] Layout wastes space and is small for a projector. The plasma is 160 to 380 px wide inside a 630 to 1190 px wide stage because the view is height-limited. At 1280x720 the KPI rail clips the last tile and the delta column is cut off. Fix: crop the view to the wall or plasma, give a 'presentation mode' with larger fonts (ticks and legend are 11.5 px, KPI labels 10.5 px) and fewer widgets, and let the table scroll horizontally or drop the unit column on narrow screens.
- 8. [MEDIUM] Not understandable within 5 seconds. The panel header 'Poloidální průřez, rovina R–Z' is jargon, there is no one-sentence message, and the legend is small. Fix: a plain Czech caption such as 'Černá = skutečný tvar plazmatu, červená = předpověď sítě z 24 čidel', a colour-coded label directly on the plot, and hide or grey out table groups that do not exist in the current configuration (X-bod, strike points, limiter show only dashes).
