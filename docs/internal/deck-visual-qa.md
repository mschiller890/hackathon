# Visual QA of `presentation/deck/slides.pptx` (projector, dim room, sceptical jury)

Reviewer: cloud session, branch `cloud/deck-visual-qa`, Fri 23 Oct 2026 about 20:00 CEST, against main 2fb3f86. Nothing in the deck, the charts or the scripts was edited; every fix below is for the owner (lane K for `presentation/deck/build_deck.py`, Lead for `presentation/pitch-script.md`).

**Method.** `soffice --headless --convert-to pdf`, `pdftoppm -r 80`; the two hidden screens (5, 10) are skipped by the PDF export, so a copy with the `show="0"` attribute removed was rendered (the deck file itself is untouched). Every one of the 14 screen images, all 11 `results/*.png` and all 11 `presentation/assets/*.png` were looked at with the Read tool. Fonts, chart axes, notes and word counts were also read from the `.pptx` with python-pptx 1.0.2 (shape sizes, `c:delete`, `c:min`, `c:logBase`). Contrast ratios are computed from the deck palette (WCAG formula).
**Limits.** Rendered by LibreOffice with Liberation Sans (metric compatible with Arial, so line breaks should match PowerPoint, but check once in PowerPoint on the projector laptop). No real projector was available. Numbers were checked against `results/ipp1-inputs.json`, `results/ipp1-latency.md`, `results/ipp1-final.md` and the speaker notes in the `.pptx`; no raw data exists in this mirror.

Deck screens are numbered 1 to 14 as in `slides.pptx` (screens 5 and 10 are hidden backups). Sizes are what the file says, in points on a 13.33 in wide slide: 14 pt tile text is about 1.7 percent of the slide width, readable at arm's length but not from the back row of a classroom.

## The five fixes that matter most for the impression

1. **Make the text bigger and the slides emptier (screens 7, 8, 11, 12, 13, 14).** Visible words: screen 7 149, screen 13 120, screen 11 117, screen 12 108, screen 14 94, screen 8 93, against the limit of about 40. Real content sits at 11 to 13 pt: screen 7 has a 75 word block at 11 pt, screen 8 a 30 word block at 10 pt, screen 11 a 77 word block at 13 pt, screen 13 blocks of 25, 35 and 14 words at 11 to 13 pt, screen 12 a 47 word block at 12 pt. Every chart axis label is 12 pt (10 pt on the left chart of screen 13) and every data label 11 pt. Fix: in `build_deck.py` move every 11 to 13 pt block to the speaker notes or to a hidden backup slide (screen 4 lines 331 and 338, screen 7 lines 392 to 402, screen 8 lines 429 to 432, screen 11 lines 470 to 478, screen 12 lines 503 to 508, screen 13 lines 525 to 527 and 553 to 556); keep one sentence of at most 18 words per slide at 18 pt or more; set chart fonts to 16 pt (`style_chart` line 219, default `size=12`; data labels `label_points` line 234, called with 11 or 12) and tile descriptions to 16 pt (`tile` line 167, `size=14`).
2. **Screen 13: the failure of the selected model is a 11 pt grey footnote.** "Vybraný model na zašuměných vstupech selže (osa 190 mm)" is the most important caveat of the talk, because screens 7, 12 and 14 headline the selected model, and it is the smallest, dimmest text on screen 13 (lines 525 to 527, 11 pt, `MUTED`). A sceptic who reads it late will think it was hidden. Fix: make it a third tile in the style of screen 7 (`tile(...)` with `"190 mm"` at 32 pt in `ORANGE` and the label "osa, vybraný model, zašuměné vstupy; síť učená se šumem 2,38 mm"), and drop the "Učeno se šumem, test bez šumu: 2,22 mm" and the 25 word "Vynechání celé rodiny tvarů" lines to the notes. The viewer still `presentation/assets/viewer-noise.png` (290,23 mm on discharge 85) is the matching backup picture and is not used anywhere.
3. **Charts have no value axis, no axis titles and no unit on the axis (screens 5, 7, 8, 12, 13).** `c.value_axis.visible = False` (lines 355, 392, 424, 497, 523, 551: all six charts) and no `minimum_scale` is set, so the viewer cannot tell that bars start at zero, and the unit "mm" lives only in a 12 pt caption above. Fix: set `c.value_axis.minimum_scale = 0` on all five charts (PowerPoint auto-scaling may not start at 0, LibreOffice did here), and put the unit into the chart title text at 16 pt ("Průměrná chyba osy R (mm)") instead of a separate caption; keep the data labels (they replace the axis). Also: screen 8's line (line 417 to 418) uses 9 categories 2, 3, 4, 5, 6, 8, 12, 16, 24 at equal spacing with a connecting line, so the slope between 6 and 12 is drawn as if it were one step; switch it to `XL_CHART_TYPE.XY_SCATTER_LINES` with real x values, like screen 13 does (line 536), or to bars. Screen 13 right chart: the log axis prints only 5 and 50 and the last point at 119 lies past the last label, so write the five real values 6, 12, 30, 60, 119 as text boxes under the axis; the label "1,47" sits on the line (move it to `XL_LABEL_POSITION.RIGHT` as screen 8 does for k in (3, 4), lines 420 to 423; labels set at line 543). "R" and "Z" in "osa R", "bod X R", "dotyk 1 R" are never explained on any slide: add to screen 6 "R vodorovně, Z svisle" (line 369).
4. **Text runs out of its tile on five screens.** `tile()` (line 162) gives the label a box of height `h * 0.4 - 0.2` and the second or third line spills across the bottom edge of the card: screen 3 tile 2 ("protažení"), screen 5 both tiles, screen 7 tiles 1 and 2 (the line "výbojů)" touches the edge), screen 12 both tiles (3 lines of 14 pt in a 2.0 in card), screen 14 "Přesnost" card (5 lines of 14 pt, last line on the edge). Fix: shorten each label to one line of at most 40 characters (the numbers are the point; the rest goes into the notes) or raise the card heights (screen 3 lines 313 to 315 to 1.5 in; screen 7 lines 376 to 381 to 1.7 in; screen 12 lines 484 to 489 to 2.3 in; screen 14 lines 578 to 579 `box(..., 1.85)` to 2.3 and shrink the text to the 40 word budget).
5. **Three numbers or captions that disagree with each other (credibility).** (a) Screen 8 shows 0,066 in the subtitle and the tile "jen 16 cívek", but the chart label at 16 inputs reads 0,067. They are two different runs (`IPR only (16)` 0.0666 as the fixed 16 coils in `results/ipp1-inputs.md` line 16 against `top 16` 0.0667 selected per fold in `ipp1-inputs.json` line 125), so on screen they look like a typo. Fix: label the tile "jen 16 cívek (pevná sada)" and the chart point "16 nejlepších vstupů", or use the same run in both (lines 410 to 428). (b) The notes of screen 9 begin "v ukázce je základní síť, ne vybraný model", while the slide and the viewer say vybraný model; the line is the `Hlavní věta:` at `presentation/pitch-script.md` line 94 (Lead's file), copied into the notes by `notes_text` (`build_deck.py` line 105). Fix: "Skutečný tvar a odhadnuté body vedle sebe; v ukázce je vybraný model na 30 odložených výbojích; jedna typická a jedna špatná ukázka." (c) The hidden screen 10 caption says all three stills are "Výboj 85", but the third (`viewer-rough-end.png`) is discharge 147 with axis error 33,69 mm, the bad end of the talk. Fix line 459: "Začátek a střed: výboj 85, typický. Konec: výboj 147, špatný konec výboje (osa 33,7 mm)."

## For the owner: order of work

The deck is built from `build_deck.py` and every item above has a line; the quickest order for one person with about 90 minutes is fix 4 (tile geometry, one edit in `tile()` plus four heights), then fix 5 (three strings), then fix 3 (axis minimum, scatter for screen 8, R and Z), then fix 2, and last fix 1 (moving small blocks into the notes), because that one also changes the speaker's timing: re-run the timing check in `presentation/REHEARSAL.md` afterwards. After each rebuild re-render with `soffice --headless --convert-to pdf presentation/deck/slides.pptx` and `pdftoppm -r 80 -png` and compare with these notes.
